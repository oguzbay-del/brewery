# Turkish preview: glossary and rules

The Turkish site is a preview at `/tr/`. It is not indexed (owner decision, 19 Nov 2026).

## Rules

- Every `/tr/` page has `<html lang="tr">` and `<meta name="robots" content="noindex, nofollow" />`.
- Do not add `/tr/` routes to `sitemap.xml`. Do not add `hreflang` alternates on English pages.
- Every `/tr/` page sets `canonical` and `og:url` to its own `/tr/` URL.
- Translate meaning, not words. Use a natural, technical Turkish register. Address the reader as "siz".
- Do not add claims, numbers, prices, customers or features. Document `web-stratejisi/41-gristora-public-marketing-claims-guardrails.md` applies to the Turkish copy too.
- Do not translate: GRISTORA, Brewing Intelligence, BeerXML, BeerJSON, BeerSmith, Brewfather, Brewer's Friend, BJCP, Brewers Association, BIAB, OG, FG, IBU, SRM, EBC, ABV, pH, CO₂, file names and units.
- Keep the URL slugs in English (`/tr/breweries/`, `/tr/features/recipe-design/`).
- Shared header and footer: pass `lang="tr"` to `SiteHeader` and `SiteFooter`.

## Terms

Use "demleme" only in "demleme günü" (brew day). For brewing in general, use "bira üretimi" or "bira yapımı". Do not use "fermantasyon" for brewing in general. Fermentation is one stage only. (Owner decision, 19 Nov 2026.)
