## 6. Decision and restrictions

**Current recommendation: conditional hold — do not claim the name is cleared and do not launch or purchase a domain yet.** This is a process status, not a legal no-go finding.

Before relying on the mark:
1. Capture official register results with application/registration numbers, owner, status, dates, classes and full goods/services.
2. Search exact, close spelling, phonetic and conceptual variants in each territory.
3. Review live, pending, expired and abandoned records and relevant adjacent classes.
4. Obtain qualified trademark counsel's written opinion on registrability and freedom-to-operate risk.
5. Capture timestamped RDAP/registrar evidence for each candidate domain.
6. Obtain explicit owner approval before any filing, purchase, contact or public clearance claim.

The existing weekly GRISTORA monitor remains the only recurring monitoring process; do not create a duplicate automation.
