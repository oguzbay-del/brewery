# GRISTORA logo vector refinement — review draft

**Status:** Owner-selected direction traced into editable SVG assets for review. This is not a production-approved master; Oğuz's approval is still required before public use.

## Source and method

- Visual source: the Library brand-board image **Painel de Marca Gristora: Inteligência na Cerveja.png**, created 15 November 2026.
- The emblem is traced from the large first/hero mark on the left of the board. Later logo-option explorations were not used.
- The G silhouette and all three amber grain forms are editable SVG paths, traced from the source mark at a 130 × 130 viewBox.
- The wordmark and descriptor remain SVG text in the board's Plus Jakarta Sans direction, with common sans-serif fallbacks. Final custom letterform outlines and optical kerning still need owner review.
- Palette: Forest Green `#0B2F26`, Amber Gold `#F4A261`, Stone Beige `#F7F6F3`, Slate Gray `#475569`.
