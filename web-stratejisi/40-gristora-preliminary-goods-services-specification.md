# GRISTORA — Preliminary goods/services specification for counsel review

**Date:** 15 November 2026  
**Status:** Draft for trademark counsel; not legal advice and not filing-ready.  
**Purpose:** Translate the current product/business scope into a narrow, defensible starting point for Nice Classification review.

## 1. Product assumptions to confirm against actual planned use

- GRISTORA is a brewery-focused software product.
- The primary audience is professional breweries; homebrewers are secondary.
- Commercial model is self-service software access.
- The marketing brand does not imply ERP, sales/order processing, shipping, accounting, CRM, keg logistics or third-party business-management consultancy.
- A downloadable mobile application may be part of the branded offering; counsel should confirm whether it is distributed under the GRISTORA mark and in which territories.
- The company is not currently described as offering software-development services to third parties under the GRISTORA mark.

## 2. Preliminary class map

| Nice class | Potential fit | Working view |
|---|---|---|
| 42 | SaaS, providing online non-downloadable software, hosting and maintenance of the branded platform | **Core / likely** if the service is hosted software. |
| 9 | Downloadable computer software and mobile applications | **Conditional / likely if** a branded downloadable app or software package is distributed. |
| 35 | Business management, business administration and commercial consultancy services | **Do not include by default.** Consider only if GRISTORA itself provides these services, not merely software that breweries use to manage their own operations. |
| 41 | Training and educational services | Consider only if GRISTORA will provide branded training/education as a standalone service, not simply help content or onboarding incidental to software. |

## 3. Draft wording for counsel to refine

### Class 42 — core hosted software
Potential starting language:
- Software as a service (SaaS) featuring software for brewery production planning and management.
- Providing online, non-downloadable software for brewing recipe formulation, brewing calculations, batch and fermentation record management, and production data analysis.
- Hosting of online software platforms for brewery production and process information management.

Use only functions that are genuinely within the current product scope and planned branded service. Remove any term that implies functionality not yet intended or supported.

### Class 9 — downloadable software, if applicable
Potential starting language:
- Downloadable computer software for brewery production planning and management.
- Downloadable software for brewing recipe formulation, brewing calculations, batch and fermentation record management, and production data analysis.
- Downloadable mobile applications for brewery production and process information management.

If the app is not distributed under the GRISTORA mark, or is not a branded deliverable in a target territory, do not include these terms without counsel's advice.

### Class 35 — hold pending business-model evidence
Class 35 covers services involving business management, operation, organisation and administration of a commercial or industrial enterprise. The fact that a software tool supports a brewery's internal management does not automatically mean the software provider offers Class 35 business-management services.

Do not claim business consultancy, outsourced business administration, business intelligence consultancy, retail/wholesale or marketplace services unless these are actual branded offerings.

### Class 41 — optional future service
Consider only if there will be separately marketed, branded training courses, workshops or educational services. Ordinary documentation, support articles and software onboarding do not automatically justify adding Class 41.

## 5. Decisions for counsel / owner

1. Confirm whether the GRISTORA mark will be used on a downloadable iOS/mobile application in each target territory.
2. Confirm whether GRISTORA is purely hosted SaaS or includes a downloadable desktop/mobile client.
3. Confirm whether branded training is a separately sold service.
4. Keep Class 35 out unless actual service delivery supports it.
5. Have counsel refine accepted local terms for US, UK, Australia and Madrid/WIPO filings and assess related classes and earlier marks.
6. Confirm intended trademark owner entity before any filing.
