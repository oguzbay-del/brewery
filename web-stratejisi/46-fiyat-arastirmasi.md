# O8 — Home and Business price research

Checked on 25 November 2026. This is input for the owner’s price decision. The options are examples for review. The owner has not selected a price.

## Method

The tables show public prices in the currency shown by the source. “Not shown” means that the opened source did not give a usable amount or term. “Not public” means the source asks the buyer to request a price. A dash in a currency column does not mean that the product is unavailable in that market. No exchange rate is used.

Annual prices are divided by 12 to show a monthly equivalent. This is an arithmetic comparison, not a monthly payment offer. Taxes, features, user limits, contract terms and market coverage can differ. Check the actual checkout or quote before a decision.
