## #210 (kind) — probable cause
The namespace enforces Pod Security `restricted`, and your containers have `readOnlyRootFilesystem: true` and `runAsUser: 1000`. The two crashing pods need writable paths:
- **objectstore (SeaweedFS):** the `emptyDir` at `/data` is owned by root. Add `fsGroup: 1000` to the pod `securityContext`. Also mount an `emptyDir` at `/tmp` (SeaweedFS writes temp files there).
- **mailpit:** mount an `emptyDir` at `/tmp` and add `fsGroup: 1000`. Mailpit writes its database to a temp file when `MP_DATABASE` is not set.
Do not remove `readOnlyRootFilesystem`. If a pod still crashes, the scope may include adding `kubectl logs --previous` for failing pods to `altyapi/k8s/kind-test.sh` (only the failure output). That change is allowed for #203.
